    About this prototype

    A scripted design concept, not an actual Siri or Spotify feature. The wallpaper is an original iPhone style recreation. The two recorded voices represent a user and the assistant. Listening history and song metadata are fictional. The original instrumental samples stand in for songs and do not contain Korean vocals. Your commands are not recorded or sent to a server. Assistant replies use prerecorded ElevenLabs audio. Test while stationary.

    This version explores one tentative combination of questions and previews. It does not replace the planned comparison of three design alternatives.
