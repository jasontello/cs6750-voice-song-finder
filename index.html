    About this prototype

    A scripted design concept, not an actual Siri or Spotify feature. The wallpaper is an original iPhone style recreation. The two recorded voices represent a user and the assistant. Listening history is simulated. Song titles and cover artwork identify real releases by SE SO NEON. Local testing uses five second excerpts from Apple Music previews. If local excerpts are unavailable, synthesized tones stand in for the recordings. Audio excerpts remain local and are not included in the public repository. Fallback instrumental tones are original. Your commands are not recorded or sent to a server. Conversation audio uses prerecorded ElevenLabs voices. Test while stationary.

    Cover artwork and release metadata: Nonadaptation and Summer Plumage. Artwork belongs to its respective rights holders.

    This version explores one tentative combination of questions and previews. It does not replace the planned comparison of three design alternatives.
